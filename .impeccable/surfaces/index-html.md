# Surface: MarkDrift tool (index.html)

Mode: Operate. The visitor came to stamp a name or logo on one file or a batch and download it.

Audience and job: creators and small businesses, often non-technical, often on a phone. They type a name, drop files, and save. Most never open advanced settings.

Constraints: UptoSolve world (white, #F7F7F5 band, ink #23231E, yellow #FCEC45, Google Sans Flex self-hosted, radii 10/18/99). Everything client-side. All existing IDs and behaviours used by tests stay working.

Memorable moment: every file in the cart already shows your mark, live, whatever its shape.

Unresolved: final product name (MarkDrift vs UptoSolve Watermark), hosting path.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A checkout, not an editor. Files sit in a cart, and one short order panel says exactly what will happen before the one button. It refuses the default editor, with a big canvas and a wall of equal-weight sliders.

OWN-WORLD: UptoSolve paper white with #F7F7F5 bands and ink #23231E. The preview stage is an ink rounded plate at 18px. Yellow #FCEC45 appears only on the primary pill, the selected style and progress. One family, Google Sans Flex, with tabular figures. Controls are native and styled from the palette, and icons are single-weight line SVG.

STORY: I see my own mark moving on a sample right away. I type my name, pick a style from five plain rows, and drop my videos. The summary reads "4 videos · 0:31 · saves to a folder you pick". I press "Watermark 4 videos" and watch each row tick to Saved.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the wordmark, "by UptoSolve", the Video/Photos pill switch and a lock line. On the left (about 62%), the ink preview plate is the drop target: a sample frame with the live mark, a yellow "Choose videos" pill in its lower third, and "or drop files anywhere" under it. The cart list sits below. On the right (400px, sticky order panel): "Your watermark" text field, five style rows with animated path diagrams, Size and Opacity, a collapsed "More options", then the summary and the full-width yellow pill at the bottom.

FORM: Checkout / order panel, #6 of my 7 ranked structures, seed key 01caa5e4. Folded in from the other dealt cards: the order panel's own step sequence (guided steps) and a summary that reads as a sentence (sentence builder).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
